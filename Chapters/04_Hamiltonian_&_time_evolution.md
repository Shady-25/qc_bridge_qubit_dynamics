# Hamiltonian and time evolution in quatum system

Classically, a system evolves because forces act upon it.

In quantum mechanics, a system evolves because the phases of its energy components rotate (more to come about this 'rotation').

examples on temporal evolution:

- an electron changes its probability distribution around the nucleus,
- a spin rotates in a magnetic field,
- a wave packet moves,
- a *qubit* oscillate between $\ket0$ & $\ket1$.

All of this is entirely determined by:
$$ \boxed{\ket{\psi(t)}=e^{-i\hbar H/t} \ket{\psi(0)}}$$
