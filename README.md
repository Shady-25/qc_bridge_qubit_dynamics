# From Schrödinger Equation to Qubit Dynamics  
*A minimal quantum computing bridge*

Qubits are **two-level quantum systems**.  
To understand the concept of qubits and related quantum computing notions, we start from the **fundamental postulates of quantum mechanics** and progressively specialize them to finite-dimensional systems relevant for quantum information.

This project builds a conceptual bridge between:
- wave functions, operators, and Hamiltonians in standard quantum mechanics, and  
- state vectors, unitary evolution, and measurement in the quantum computing framework.

The focus is on **non-relativistic, closed quantum systems only**.  
Open quantum systems, noise, decoherence, and Lindblad-type dynamics are deliberately excluded.
